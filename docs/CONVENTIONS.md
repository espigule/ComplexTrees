# Mathematical and display conventions

DD, DO and OO are plane orientations, not endpoint-reversal signatures. The selected figures use DD(1,0), DD(1,1), DO(1,0), DO(1,1).

S1(z)=-1+a1 J1(z), S2(z)=1+a2 J2(z), with J(z)=z or conjugate(z). The translation constants are not generally fixed points. Exterior coefficients c1=1/a1 and c2=1/a2 have moduli greater than one. The mixed normalization retains conjugations.

Every zipper is connected in its contraction domain. Its canonical curve is embedded only when there are no additional identifications. Coloured sheets show positive stable coverage; unpainted regions have no assigned classification. Finite search survival is not a connectedness certificate. The toroidal display is a coordinate model; its central hole is not a topological claim about the locus. An island in a zipper slice does not itself prove a cavity of the full four-dimensional connectedness locus.

Shared probes: qA=1/2+i/5, qB=1/2+2i/5, qC=0.34090875+0.43484625i. Their stability classifications and geometric error bounds are separate records, not inferred from appearance.
