4,096 certified cells around the island probe, magnified in the c₁ plane. The circular C marker in the atlas locates this patch; its symbol size is independent of the patch size.

Axes show c₁ − c₁(C), in units of 10⁻⁶.

Each sheet approximates the nonlinear image of a certified inner cover with a triangular mesh. Color and the weight filter retain the fourth coordinate w; the three-dimensional view projects it out. Blank space carries no classification. Apparent intersections between projected sheets need not be intersections in four dimensions.

Coordinates of the projection
c₁ = 1/a, c₂ = 1/b;   ρ = 2/(|a|+|b|);   w = |a|/(|a|+|b|).
θ₁ = arg(a), θ₂ = arg(b).
X = (4 + ρ cos θ₁) cos θ₂
Y = (4 + ρ cos θ₁) sin θ₂
Z = ρ sin θ₁

At fixed w and orientation, this chart is injective on the displayed range 0 < ρ ≤ 2. DD and DO belong to separate orientation charts. Its central hole comes from the display map. The faint grid is the reference torus ρ = 2. The slider selects a finite band w ± 0.025 around the centres of displayed cells.
